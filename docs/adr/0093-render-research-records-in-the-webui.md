# Render Research Records in the WebUI

The Local Operator Console will open every Research Record on a dedicated detail view. A passed or partial Record safely renders its Markdown Team Report in place and shows the exact Team version, Research Subject, request time, Research Boundary, and evidence quality. A blocked, failed, or cancelled Record instead shows its stopping stage and bounded reason. A filesystem path alone is not an adequate report surface, and the view never exposes raw logs, prompts, private model reasoning, or Codex session material.
