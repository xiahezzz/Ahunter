# Require minute evidence for formal LAgent fills

LAgent's premarket-only decision regime still requires an evidence-based account of standing orders executed during the trading day. Formal ordinary limit-order evaluation therefore requires historical minute data at minimum, while limit-up queues and other cases remain subject to any finer evidence required by the fixed fill model. This increases historical data requirements over daily-bar simulation but prevents price-range shortcuts from being presented as credible fills; minute data itself is not proof of counterfactual execution, and missing required evidence remains a quality failure.
