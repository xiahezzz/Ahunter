# Never aggregate or compare conclusions across research teams

Each Research Team will publish its own Team Conclusion because different Agent membership represents a different investment style. A Research Cycle may list Team identity, run status, and artifact locations for navigation, but the Engine and reporting layer will not calculate or narrate agreement, disagreement, evidence overlap, ranking, consensus, or any cross-Team action. No comparison artifact, meta-model, or deterministic cross-Team analysis will be introduced.
