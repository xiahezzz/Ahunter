# Block incomplete research teams independently

Every Research Agent named by a Research Team is required for that team version. A missing Data Product, failed Codex task, timeout, or invalid Research Finding blocks that Research Run before the Decision Pipeline, while other teams continue independently; optional members and silent partial execution were rejected because they would change a team's investment style without changing its identity. Successful intermediate findings remain available for diagnosis but cannot support a published team conclusion.
