# Compose Market Team Conclusions from typed Market Insights

A Market Team Conclusion will use one stable evidence, quality, risk, invalidation, and time-horizon envelope containing one or more typed Market Insights, which may cover market conditions, sentiment, industries, themes, styles, or selected securities. The versioned Market Decision Pipeline owns the allowed Insight kinds and their schemas; Teams may emit any contracted combination but cannot invent output shapes. A selected security is only an evidence-linked Research Candidate: it carries no Security Decision Stance, price range, or position guidance and never starts a child Research Request automatically. This supersedes ADR-0022's cross-Scope requirement that every Team Conclusion carry a five-level security stance; that contract remains specific to Security Team Conclusions. Requiring one Market Regime was rejected because Market Teams may answer materially different market-level questions, while Team-defined or unconstrained free-form reports were rejected because their evidence and output shape could not be validated, versioned, or rendered reliably.
