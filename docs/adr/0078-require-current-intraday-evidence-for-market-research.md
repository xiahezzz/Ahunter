# Require current intraday evidence for market research during trading

A Market Team requested during an active Shanghai-Shenzhen trading session must use a sealed Whole-Market Intraday Snapshot observed at or before its Research Boundary; outside active trading it may use the latest completed session. If the required intraday snapshot is missing or stale, affected market conclusions block instead of presenting the previous close as current market sentiment or sector strength.
