# Vary research teams only by agent membership

Investment styles will be represented by named, versioned Research Teams that differ only in Research Agent membership. Every team will pass through the same Decision Pipeline, so synthesis, Trader behavior, risk review, final adjudication, models, and publication policy do not vary by team; team-specific pipelines were rejected because they would confound agent-selection effects and multiply the contracts that must be validated.
