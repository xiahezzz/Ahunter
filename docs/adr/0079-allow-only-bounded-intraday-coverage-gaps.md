# Allow only bounded gaps in whole-market intraday coverage

A Whole-Market Intraday Snapshot may support Market Insights when unresolved observations are at most 1% of the expected market and every sector retains at least 95% effective coverage; evidenced suspensions and securities outside their listing interval are legitimate terminal absences rather than gaps. Reports must disclose actual coverage, and crossing either threshold blocks dependent Insights. These thresholds belong to the versioned Data Product contract so changing them cannot silently alter historical quality semantics.
