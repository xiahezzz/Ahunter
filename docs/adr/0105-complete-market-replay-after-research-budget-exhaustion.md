# Complete market replay after research budget exhaustion

Stopping an Episode's return measurement when its research allowance runs out would compare different market periods and could reward early stopping. On research budget exhaustion, stop model work and discard unsubmitted drafts, but execute already accepted day orders, cancellations, and precommitted replacements under their original rules and carry the account to the configured terminal valuation without new agent decisions or forced liquidation. Reserve the necessary environment and evaluation resources within the same total allowance before research begins, and admit the final result only if the full period, evidence, accounting, and cost requirements are satisfied; cancellation and infrastructure failure remain separate outcomes.
