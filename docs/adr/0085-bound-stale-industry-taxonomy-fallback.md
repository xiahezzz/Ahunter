# Bound stale industry-taxonomy fallback

If the current trading date's Industry Sector Taxonomy refresh cannot be sealed, sector-dependent research may pin the latest successful taxonomy no more than five trading dates old and must disclose its version and age. If no such version exists, only sector-strength and sector-rotation Market Insights block; independent market-sentiment and macro-policy Insights may still publish. Unlimited stale fallback was rejected because it could present obsolete sector membership as current, while blocking the entire Market Team Conclusion would discard unaffected evidence.
