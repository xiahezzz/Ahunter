# Rerun research as a new request

Every terminal Research Record may be used to create a new Manual Research Request that copies the predecessor's exact Team version and Research Subject. The new Request receives a new acceptance time, Scope-specific Research Boundary, and Research Data Snapshot, and records a `rerun_of` link to the predecessor. It never resumes or overwrites the prior execution, so changing evidence remains visible as a new conclusion rather than an in-place retry of history.
