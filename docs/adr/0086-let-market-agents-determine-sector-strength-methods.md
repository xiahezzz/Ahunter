# Let Market Agents determine sector-strength methods

Each Market Agent may determine how it assesses sector strength and rotation from the pinned Whole-Market Intraday Snapshot, Industry Sector Taxonomy, and other contracted evidence. Every Sector Strength Assessment must disclose a structured method summary covering inputs, time windows, strength or rotation criteria, ranking or grouping basis, and evidence references; this summary does not include prompts or private model reasoning. The Market Decision Pipeline validates the typed Market Insight and its evidence envelope but will not prescribe one shared formula, composite score, or ranking method. Engine-owned scoring was rejected because distinct Market Agents are intended to express distinct analytical approaches rather than differ only in prose around the same calculation, while undisclosed methods were rejected because users could not compare or audit conflicting assessments.
