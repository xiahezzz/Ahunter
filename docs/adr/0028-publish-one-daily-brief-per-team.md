# Publish one daily brief per Team

A Daily Research Batch will hold one time boundary, selected Team set, and Codex Execution Policy while launching an independent Research Cycle for every selected subject. After those Cycles finish, the reporting layer deterministically renders one Daily Team Brief per Team from that Team's existing Team Reports and blocked statuses. The brief does not call Codex again, rank subjects, or create another conclusion. No global daily summary or cross-Team daily document is produced.
