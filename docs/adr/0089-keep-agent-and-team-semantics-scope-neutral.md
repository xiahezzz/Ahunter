# Keep Agent and Team semantics scope-neutral

Market and Security Agents and Teams will share one identity, Manifest, versioning, publication, Agent Data Access, Run Capsule, Research Query Interface, execution, retry, audit, and lifecycle model. Research Scope is their only categorical distinction: it selects the Research Subject and the directly compatible Data Product and conclusion contracts required for that subject. Scope-specific copies of otherwise identical Agent or Team rules are rejected because they would create two systems whose behavior could drift. Accordingly, declared historical market products expose all locally retained evidence visible at the Research Boundary, and either kind of Agent chooses its working lookback under the same bounded, audited query rules rather than receiving an engine-fixed 5-day or 20-day window.
