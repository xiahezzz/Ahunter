# Prioritize official market information

The Market Information Product used by `market_macro_policy@1` will prioritize original releases from competent public authorities, regulators, exchanges, and statistical institutions. Financial media may supplement event discovery and market context, but when an original publication is available the evidence must reference it, and an unverified rumor cannot be represented as fact. The Research Engine owns source normalization, time-bound validation, provenance, and deduplication; the Agent owns interpretation of whole-market implications.
