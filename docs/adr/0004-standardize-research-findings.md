# Standardize research findings without erasing specialization

Every Research Agent will return a Research Finding with a common core contract for identity, time boundary, summary, claims, evidence, risks, invalidation conditions, and quality, plus agent-specific structured details. Fully arbitrary outputs were rejected because they would require per-agent aggregation code, while one exhaustive shared schema was rejected because it would force unrelated specialties into fields they do not naturally own.
