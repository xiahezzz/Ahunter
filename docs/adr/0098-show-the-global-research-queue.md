# Show the global research queue

The Local Operator Console's current-research area will show the single running Research Record followed by every queued Record in actual execution order. Each row displays the exact Team, Research Scope, Research Subject, trigger origin, durable phase, bounded progress, and available cancellation action. The view is global and does not inherit filters from Team selection or the Research Report Explorer, because hidden pending work would make a single system-wide execution queue misleading.
